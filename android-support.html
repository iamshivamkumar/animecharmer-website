Android player support

Help with the campaign, map downloads and your saved progress.

Updated 18 October 2026
Android package: com.animecharmer.lastarmourfall

Contact Anime Charmer

Email iam.shivamsng@gmail.com with the subject “Last Armourfall Android Support”. No support account is required.

For a bug report, include:

  • Your device model and Android version.
  • The Last Armourfall version.
  • The operation number or menu name.
  • What you expected, what happened, and whether it repeats.

Screenshots or logs are optional. Hide personal information before sending, and do not send passwords.

Campaign map downloads

The first 10 operations are included with the game. Later operations download free through Google Play Asset Delivery as you progress. Map downloads require an internet connection and enough storage. Operations whose content is already downloaded can be played offline.

Automatic downloads start on Wi-Fi by default. You can allow mobile data or choose Download Now. If a map download is unavailable, include the operation number and the on-screen message in your support report.

Optional rewarded ads

Rewarded ads are optional and available only to players who confirm they are 18 or older. Ads require an internet connection and availability. Ordinary gameplay remains available without watching them. Ad Privacy Options are provided in the credits menu where applicable.

If a reward is not applied after an ad completes, include the reward type, operation and any on-screen message when contacting support.

Saves and privacy

The game saves progress on your device. Restarting normally keeps your progress. Clearing app data or uninstalling can erase local saves; do not do this as a first troubleshooting step. We cannot restore a save that we do not hold. Operating-system backups, where available, must be managed separately.

For access or deletion requests relating to information you sent us, email the same address with the subject “Last Armourfall Android Privacy”. Read the Android privacy policy for details.
