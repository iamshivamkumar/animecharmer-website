Quest privacy policy

How information is handled when you play Last Armourfall VR or contact Anime Charmer.

Updated 22 November 2026
Quest package: com.animecharmer.lastarmourfallvr

Shivam Kumar, operating as Anime Charmer in India, is responsible for the data Last Armourfall VR processes. This policy covers the Quest game, including test builds. The Android edition has a separate privacy policy.

Progress and settings

The game stores campaign progress, earned credits, unlocked units, upgrades, paint and control/audio preferences on your device. These saves make progression and preferences persist. The game has no Anime Charmer account or developer-operated cloud-save service.

Headset and controllers

Headset and controller poses, button presses and stick movement are processed on your device to position your view, aim and control the tank. Test builds can write local diagnostic logs, including input and performance information. The game does not automatically upload these logs to Anime Charmer.

Optional purchases on Meta Quest

In versions that support paid unlocks, Last Armourfall VR collects and processes purchase information from Meta for the signed-in player. The game reads the product identifiers (SKUs) in the list of purchases supplied by Meta to determine which of the tank, vehicle and infantry packs the player owns. It uses these ownership records, called entitlements, to unlock the corresponding units, show which packs are owned, and restore access after reinstalling with the same Meta account.

The garage requests product identifiers and localized prices from Meta to display the available packs. Selecting a pack sends its identifier to Meta to open checkout. Meta handles payment and maintains the account purchase record. The game reads the updated ownership list after checkout. It processes ownership on the headset and can read a device-cached entitlement supplied by Meta when the online lookup fails. Anime Charmer does not keep a separate server database of Quest purchases or receive payment card details through the game.

Online services

This VR build contains no advertising service, real-money credit purchases or developer analytics service. Anime Charmer does not sell game data or share it for targeted advertising. Meta operates the headset and store under its own privacy policy; any platform diagnostics or platform-managed backups are governed by Meta.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the message, attachments or logs you choose to send. We use them to answer you, investigate problems and handle privacy requests. Email providers process messages to deliver and store them. Do not send passwords or sensitive account information.

How to request deletion of your data

Email iam.shivamsng@gmail.com with the subject “Last Armourfall VR data deletion”. Say which data you want deleted, such as support messages, diagnostic logs you sent, or purchase-related information you shared with us. You can make a request even if you have uninstalled the game. We may ask for information needed to identify your records and verify the request. Please leave out passwords and payment card details.

We will delete the requested records we control unless retention is legally required, and reply with the outcome. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. To remove local progress, settings and earned unlocks, clear the game's app data on your headset. Manage any platform backups separately, as they may restore a save.

Clearing game data leaves the purchase on your Meta account, which is how a paid pack can be restored. For deletion of purchase records held by Meta, use Meta Quest Support. Anime Charmer can help identify which data it controls if you are unsure where to send your request.

Your choices and contact

Depending on your location, you may have privacy rights, including access, correction, deletion or objection. Contact iam.shivamsng@gmail.com with the subject “Last Armourfall VR Privacy”. We may request reasonable verification. We will update this policy when practices change; check its date for the latest revision.

Platform privacy

Meta provides the Quest device, operating system and store. Read Meta's Privacy Policy for how Meta handles information through its services. This game policy does not replace Meta's policy.

For information about visiting this website, read the website privacy notice. For help with the game, visit Quest support.
