Quest privacy policy

How information is handled when you play Last Armourfall VR or contact Anime Charmer.

Updated 18 October 2026
Quest package: com.animecharmer.lastarmourfallvr

Last Armourfall VR is provided by Anime Charmer. This policy covers the Quest game, including test builds. The Android edition has a separate privacy policy.

Progress and settings

The game stores campaign progress, earned credits, unlocked units, upgrades, paint and control/audio preferences on your device. These saves make progression and preferences persist. The game has no Anime Charmer account or developer-operated cloud-save service.

Headset and controllers

Headset and controller poses, button presses and stick movement are processed on your device to position your view, aim and control the tank. Test builds can write local diagnostic logs, including input and performance information. The game does not automatically upload these logs to Anime Charmer.

Online services

This VR build contains no advertising service, real-money credit purchases or developer analytics service. Anime Charmer does not sell game data or share it for targeted advertising. Meta operates the headset and store under its own privacy policy; any platform diagnostics or platform-managed backups are governed by Meta.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the message, attachments or logs you choose to send. We use them to answer you, investigate problems and handle privacy requests. Email providers process messages to deliver and store them. Do not send passwords or sensitive account information.

Retention and deletion

Local saves remain until app data is removed. Removing them deletes progress. Device or platform backups may restore data and must be managed separately. We keep support correspondence only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. Email us to request access, correction or deletion of information you sent us.

Your choices and contact

Depending on your location, you may have privacy rights, including access, correction, deletion or objection. Contact iam.shivamsng@gmail.com with the subject “Last Armourfall VR Privacy”. We may request reasonable verification. We will update this policy when practices change; check its date for the latest revision.

Platform privacy

Meta provides the Quest device, operating system and store. Read Meta's Privacy Policy for how Meta handles information through its services. This game policy does not replace Meta's policy.

For information about visiting this website, read the website privacy notice. For help with the game, visit Quest support.
