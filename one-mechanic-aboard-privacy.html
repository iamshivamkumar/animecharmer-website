One Mechanic Aboard privacy policy

How the Quest game uses your movements, saves your progress and handles information you send to the studio.

Updated 24 November 2026

Shivam Kumar, operating as Anime Charmer in India, is responsible for this game's data practices. This policy covers One Mechanic Aboard for Meta Quest, including test builds.

During play

The headset processes headset and Touch controller positions, rotations and button input to position your view, fit the suit, move your hands and register actions such as gripping a ladder or wielding the sword. Enemies hear footsteps generated by your movement in the game. The game does not record your voice or request microphone access.

Your headset saves mission and checkpoint progress, control mappings, hand calibration and preferences locally. The game does not send your movements or progress to Anime Charmer. The studio operates no player accounts, advertising, analytics or cloud saves for this game.

Store purchases and Meta services

Meta handles purchases of the game through the Horizon Store. The game has no in-app purchases. Anime Charmer does not receive your payment card details through the game. Meta handles its store, system diagnostics and any platform-managed backups under Meta's Privacy Policy.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and any message, screenshots or logs you send. We use them to respond, troubleshoot issues and handle privacy requests. Our email provider processes them to deliver and store correspondence. We retain these records only as needed to resolve the request, follow up and meet applicable obligations. Anime Charmer does not sell this information or use it for targeted advertising.

Access or delete your data

You can remove local saves and settings by clearing this game's app data on your headset. This resets campaign progress. Manage any Quest backups separately if you want to prevent a save from being restored.

To request access, correction or deletion of information you sent the studio, email iam.shivamsng@gmail.com. Name the game and describe the information you mean. You can contact us after uninstalling. We may ask for enough information to identify your records. We will reply with the outcome and delete records we control unless retention is legally required.

For Meta account or store purchase records, contact Meta Quest Support. For information about this site, read our website privacy notice.
