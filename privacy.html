About this website

This website uses no analytics scripts, advertising tags, third-party embeds or contact-form backend. Images and the optional video are served directly with the site. The pages do not set tracking cookies.

Visiting this website sends connection information, such as your IP address and the page requested, to the hosting provider. That provider may maintain ordinary service and security logs under its own practices.

If you follow a link to Google Play, Meta, YouTube or another external service, that destination handles information under its own policies. Email links open your chosen email service. If you contact Anime Charmer, we receive your email address and the message or attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond, investigate problems and handle privacy requests.

We keep support correspondence only as needed to resolve the request, handle related follow-up and meet applicable obligations. Please avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Privacy requests

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information you have supplied to us. Email iam.shivamsng@gmail.com and tell us which game or website request your message concerns. We may ask for reasonable verification before responding to a request.

Anime Charmer does not hold a copy of your local game saves. Removing app data can erase your progress; manage any operating-system or platform backup separately.
