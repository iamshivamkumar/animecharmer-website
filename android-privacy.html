Android privacy policy

How information is handled when you play Last Armourfall or contact Anime Charmer.

Updated 24 October 2026
Android package: com.animecharmer.lastarmourfall

This policy covers Last Armourfall for Android, provided by Anime Charmer, including test versions. The Quest edition has a separate privacy policy.

Game progress on your device

The game saves credits, campaign progress, unlocked units, upgrades, surviving units and your control, audio and graphics preferences on your device. This version does not provide a game account, multiplayer chat or our own cloud-save service. We do not send your save file to a game server. Your operating system may separately back up app data depending on its settings.

Map downloads through Google Play

The first 10 operations are included with the game. Later operations download free through Google Play Asset Delivery in packs as you progress. Map downloads need an internet connection and storage space. The download service is separate from advertising and can initialize at app startup, regardless of whether ads are available to the player.

Google documents processing of device metadata and the application's version to deliver suitable assets and preserve installed packs across updates. Google states that this data is encrypted, is not transferred to third parties, and is deleted after a fixed retention period; the documentation does not specify that period's length. See Google Play Asset Delivery's data-safety information. Google Play also operates under its own terms and privacy practices. The game does not upload your date of birth or local save file to this service.

Automatic map downloads start on Wi-Fi by default. You can allow mobile data or request a download yourself. These transfer choices are separate from advertising privacy choices and do not disable the download SDK's initialization.

Intended audience and age choices

Last Armourfall is intended for players aged 13 and older. Regional content ratings may require a higher age; check the rating shown in your country. The game's age screen controls advertising eligibility, not the regional content rating.

The game asks you to choose an age group: 18+, 13–17 or Under 13. You can also choose Skip, which records your age as unknown. Only a coarse category—adult, under 18 or unknown—is saved on your device. The game does not ask for or store an exact date of birth. For players under 18 and players whose age is unknown, rewarded ads and ad-consent requests are disabled. Ordinary gameplay remains available without watching ads.

Parents or guardians should contact support on behalf of younger children. Please do not include a child's exact birth date or other unnecessary personal details. If we learn that a child has sent information that is not needed to handle a support request, we will remove it.

Optional advertisements and privacy choices

For players who declare they are 18 or older, the game uses Google AdMob rewarded ads and Google's User Messaging Platform for privacy choices. Google's advertising SDK can process IP-based approximate location, app/ad interactions, performance information, and advertising or other device/account identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Google states that it encrypts this data in transit. See Google's advertising SDK disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

Starting with version 1.0.10, the Android app also includes Meta Audience Network through AdMob mediation. Subject to service approval and availability, Meta may supply optional rewarded ads. Its SDK can process device and network information, identifiers and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads, improve its services and prevent fraud. This information may be associated with information Meta holds from other services. See Meta's Privacy Policy for its information practices and controls. The game does not include Facebook Login, request access to your Facebook profile or require a Facebook account to play.

For adult players, the consent service can refresh privacy information at startup, before an ad is requested. This request includes technical information such as device model, Android version, language, screen configuration, app version and stored consent information. Consent processing is separate from choosing to watch a rewarded ad.

Watching a rewarded ad is optional. Depending on where you live and the available privacy message, you may be asked to make advertising choices. The credits menu provides Ad Privacy Options where applicable. Android settings also provide advertising-ID controls. A privacy choice or unavailable ad does not prevent ordinary gameplay.

The same adult-only and consent checks apply to Meta ad requests. The game enables Meta's Limited Data Use option where Meta supports it. This limits certain processing under Meta's rules; it does not mean that no data is processed or replace the privacy choices offered in the game.

The Android game does not include real-money credit purchases or a developer-operated analytics service. In-game credits have no cash value.

Contacting support

If you choose to email us, we receive the email address and information you send, such as your message and any screenshots. We use this to answer your request and investigate the issue. Please avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Email providers also process that correspondence to deliver and store it under their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until removed from your device. You can clear the game's app data in Android settings or uninstall the app; this can erase progress. Copies in operating-system backups may need to be managed separately. We cannot restore a local save that we do not hold.

We retain support correspondence only while needed to resolve the request, handle related follow-up or meet applicable obligations. Contact us to ask about or request deletion of information you sent us. Google and Meta control retention of advertising-service information under their respective policies; clearing a game save does not delete their records.

Security and your choices

Game saves use the app's storage and device protections. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to withdraw consent. Contact us for requests relating to information you supplied to us. Google's and Meta's linked controls and policies apply to information each handles.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game's features or information practices change. The date above identifies the latest version.
