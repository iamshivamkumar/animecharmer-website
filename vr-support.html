Quest player support

Help with seated controls, campaign progress and your Quest experience.

Updated 18 October 2026
Quest package: com.animecharmer.lastarmourfallvr

Contact Anime Charmer

Email iam.shivamsng@gmail.com with the subject “Last Armourfall VR Support”. No support account is required.

For a bug report, include:

  • Your Quest model and system version.
  • The Last Armourfall VR version.
  • The operation number or menu name.
  • What you expected, what happened, and whether it repeats.

Screenshots or logs are optional. Hide personal information before sending, and do not send passwords.

Controls and comfort

Sit comfortably and press X to recenter with the default bindings. Point with the right controller and press its trigger to select; B goes back. Settings → VR Controls opens on the front glass. If you have changed your bindings, use the actions shown in your control settings.

If the tank moves with the stick untouched, check the controller deadzone in Quest system settings.

Graphics settings

In Settings, select Auto Detect Graphics to reapply the device rendering profile. This restores the selected headset profile; it is not a performance benchmark or a frame-rate guarantee.

Saves and privacy

Restarting the game normally keeps progress. Removing app data deletes local saves; do not do this as a first troubleshooting step. We cannot recover saves that exist only on your device.

For access or deletion requests relating to information you sent us, email the same address with the subject “Last Armourfall VR Privacy”. Read the Quest privacy policy for details.
