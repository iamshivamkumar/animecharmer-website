Controls & Comfort

Sit comfortably and press X to recenter (default binding). Point with the right controller and press its trigger to select; B goes back. Settings > VR Controls opens on the front glass. If the tank moves with the stick untouched, check the controller deadzone in Quest system settings.

Saves & Privacy

Restarting the game normally keeps progress. Removing app data deletes local saves; do not do this as a first troubleshooting step. For access or deletion requests, email the same address with the subject Last Armourfall VR Privacy. We cannot recover saves that exist only on your device.

Graphics settings

In Settings, select Auto Detect Graphics to reapply the device rendering profile. This restores the selected headset profile; it is not a performance benchmark or a frame-rate guarantee.

What to include in a report

  • Quest model and system version
  • Last Armourfall VR version
  • Operation number or menu name
  • What you expected, what happened, and whether it repeats
