One Mechanic Aboard support

Controls, training and mission help for the Quest edition.

Updated 25 November 2026

Getting started

View One Mechanic Aboard on the Meta Horizon Store for Quest 2, Quest 3 and Quest 3S. The game uses two Touch controllers. Training and the first mission are available at the start of a new campaign.

In Training, practice taking the sword from your hip with a grip button. Keep the grip held while swinging. To climb, grip a rung and pull yourself toward it while reaching for the next rung with the other hand. Check the watch on your left wrist for your health.

Controls and checkpoints

Open the in-game menu with the Menu button on the left controller. Open Settings to review your bindings and adjust sound and vibration. For help with hand alignment, include which controller and grip position appear offset.

Campaign progression and preferences are stored on the headset. An update or normal restart should retain local data. Clearing app data resets local progress, so keep it while troubleshooting a save.

How to complete a mission

Defeat the boarding captain and pull the lever at the docking connector to detach the invaders' ship. Clear the remaining enemies. An invader at the controls can reconnect the ship, so return to the lever if that happens. Securing the station unlocks the next mission.

Report a problem

Email iam.shivamsng@gmail.com with your Quest model, game version, mission or training activity, and what happened. Screenshots are optional. For questions about your data, see the One Mechanic Aboard privacy policy.
